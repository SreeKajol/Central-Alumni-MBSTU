## Image Recommendations:

- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 
  - Profile photos (alumni/authors): 400x400px to 800x800px square, headshots work best
  - Activity photos (about section): 800x800px square or landscape
  - News cover images: 800x600px or similar landscape
  - Logos: PNG with transparent background, ~200px height
- **File size**: Keep under 500KB for faster loading
- **Quality**: Use clear, professional photos with good lighting

## Image Guidelines:

**Profile Photos:**
- Should be professional headshots
- Clear face visibility
- Neutral or professional background
- Square aspect ratio (will be displayed as circular)

**News Images:**
- Related to the news topic
- High quality and relevant
- Landscape orientation preferred

**Activity Photos:**
- Show alumni engagement, events, or campus activities
- Group photos or meaningful moments
- Good lighting and composition

## How to Add Images:

1. Copy your images to this folder: `/public/images/`
2. Rename them to match the exact filenames above
3. Ensure proper file extensions (.jpg or .png)
4. Refresh your browser to see them on the website

## Note:
These images are publicly accessible at: `http://your-domain.com/images/filename.jpg`
